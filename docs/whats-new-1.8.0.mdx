# What's new in 1.8.0 - Farmlands

Farmlands turns the open land into real countryside. Until now, fields rendered as one
endless carpet of tilled crops, meadows were flat green, and the huge plains OSM never
mapped looked empty. 1.8.0 gives all of it the structure of real agricultural land —
and puts you in control of the mix.

## Field parcels

Farmland now splits into **rectangular parcels** like real plots seen from above: long
strips and blocky fields at multiple angles, **aligned to the nearby road network** the
way real cadastre follows its access roads. Parcels are separated by dirt tracks,
their interiors carry worn spots and noise texture, and field systems change
orientation along organically wandering borders instead of a grid. Parcel sizes are
defined in real metres and track the map scale, and a **Pattern size** slider
(25-400%) zooms the whole layout.

## Real crop plots

Each field grows **one crop**: wheat, potato, carrot, beetroot, sunflower rows on
tilled ridges, pumpkin patches on a grass mosaic, or fallow resting ground. Neighbour
fields ripen at different growth stages, bird-sown stray patches break up the
monoculture, wheat and fallow plots carry hay-bale bundles, and **sunflower fields
cluster in the low, open plains**. A new **Farm plots** slider group weights the crop
shares.

## Three land profiles

A **Profile** picker gives each land kind its own five-way mix (Coarse dirt / Plains /
Flowers / Farmland / Moss):

- **Farmland** — OSM-tagged fields, your main sliders.
- **Grassland** — OSM meadows, grass, orchards and village greens; defaults grassy.
- **Untagged land** — the satellite-classified plains OSM leaves blank; defaults to
  open plains rather than endless crops. Villages get grassy ground instead of wheat.

Everything carries vanilla-density cover: short grass, ferns, two-block large ferns,
tall grass, ten wildflower species, sunflowers, dead bushes, and moss.

## Rocks and bushes

One selector (Rocks + bushes / Rocks / Bushes / None) scatters 68 bundled schematics —
8 andesite/tuff rock formations and 60 bushes across 10 species — at random rotations
over farm, grass and untagged land. Bushes appear in roughly 5% of chunks and the much
rarer rocks in about 2%, never on rivers, lakes, roads, or tilled fields.

## Live pattern preview

The "🌾 Preview pattern" canvas mirrors the generator: angled parcels and strips,
domain borders, per-crop plot tints (farmland drawn golden), vegetation speckle, and
scatter dots — following whichever profile you have selected, with scroll-zoom and a
wide area stepper.

## Fixes

- Flat lowland farmland no longer gets stray snow speckle (snow needs real mountain
  relief; genuine peaks still cap).
- Nothing floats over water any more: sunflowers, flowers, moss and bushes are swept
  off rivers and lakes, and schematic pieces never stamp onto water.

Farmlands bundles the arnis fork 3.0.4 generator. Every new behaviour is additive and
seam-safe; a project that never opens the Farmland drawer builds exactly as before.
